1 — Summary

In Short

Receh is an expense tracking app. All your financial records and receipt photos are stored on your own device. The developer does not run any servers, has no user accounts, and does not receive, view, or sell your data.

Receipts are read by a text recognizer that runs on your device. Receipt photos are not sent over the internet to be read.

Backup to Google Drive is optional and is stored in your own Google Drive account, in a hidden folder reserved for Receh. Receh cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Since version 1.0.3, Receh shows native ads from Google AdMob on the Home, History, and Report screens, always labeled "Ad". AdMob receives the advertising ID, device data, and ad interactions; your financial records, receipt photos, and backup contents are never shared with AdMob (see section 5).

2 — Data Stored

Data Stored

The following data is kept in Receh's private storage on your device, and contains only what you enter yourself:

Financial records

Transactions

Amount, type (expense/income), category, wallet, store name, note, date and time.

Financial records

Line items

Item name, quantity, unit price, and subtotal from receipts you scan or type in.

Photos

Receipt photos

Only if you choose to keep them. This can be turned off per transaction or for all scans.

Settings

App preferences

Language, currency, number format, theme, and the categories and wallets you create.

Optional

Google account info

If you sign in with Google: email address, name, and profile photo URL, shown in the app. Stored on your device only.

What Receh does not store or collect

  • Location, contacts, SMS, call history, or the list of installed apps.
  • Bank account or card numbers, PINs, or banking and e-wallet passwords.
  • Any photos in your gallery other than the receipt photos you pick.
  • Any financial data for advertising purposes. The advertising ID is read only by the Google AdMob SDK (section 5), not by the developer.

3 — Use

How Data Is Used

Your data is used only to run the app's features on your device:

  • Showing your transaction history, summaries, and reports (by category, by day, most frequently bought items, and so on).
  • Auto-filling the amount, date, store, and items from a receipt photo.
  • Suggesting categories based on stores you have recorded before. This learning stays on your device.
  • Creating and restoring backups, if you use them.

No user profiles, credit scoring, or marketing are built from your data.

4 — Storage & Backup

Storage & Backup

By default your data exists only on your device. Data leaves your device only in the following ways:

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Google, Receh stores backup files in the hidden app data folder (appDataFolder) of your Google Drive account, using the drive.appdata permission. This permission only grants access to files created by Receh itself; Receh cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.

  • Backups contain transactions, line items, saved receipt photos, and app settings.
  • Backups are made automatically after changes and when the app is closed, with intervals that keep data usage low. This can be turned off.
  • Only the 5 most recent backups are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • Backup files count toward your Google Drive storage quota and are subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Android Auto Backup

Android may back up app data to your device's Google account according to your device backup settings, and restore it when you set up a new device. For Receh, this cloud backup includes only the transaction database and settings, without receipt photos. Receipt photos are only included in a direct device-to-device transfer (cable or Wi-Fi) when setting up a new device.

Export to file

You can export a backup as a .receh file and save or send it anywhere you choose. Once exported, the file is entirely under your control.

Safety copy when restoring

Before the data on your device is replaced by a backup, Receh keeps one copy of the existing data in the app's private storage so that a mistaken restore can be undone.

6 — Permissions

App Permissions

  • Camera and gallery — opened only when you choose to take or pick a receipt photo. Receh receives only the photos you take or select.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — added by the AdMob SDK for ads; can be reset or deleted in Android settings.
  • Internet — only for Google sign-in and Google Drive backup, ML Kit communication, and loading AdMob ads, as described in section 5. Recording, scanning, and reports keep working without internet.

7 — Security

Data Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which Android prevents other apps from accessing. Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Receh never asks for or stores your Google account password; signing in is handled entirely by Google.

Data security also depends on your device. We recommend using a screen lock and protecting your Google account with 2-Step Verification.

8 — Your Control

Your Rights & Choices

Viewing and changing data

All data can be viewed, edited, and deleted directly in the app.

Deleting data

  • Transactions or categories: delete them directly in the app.
  • Google Drive backups: delete them one by one in Settings → Backup & restore, or delete them all from Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Receh → Delete hidden app data.
  • Google Drive access: choose Disconnect in the app, or revoke Receh's access at myaccount.google.com/connections. Existing backups stay in Drive until you delete them.
  • All data on the device: uninstall Receh, or clear the app's data in Android Settings.
  • Android Auto Backup: manage it through the Google backup settings on your device.

Because the developer does not keep your data on any server, there is no data held by the developer that needs to be requested for deletion. A full guide, including data retention periods, is on the Delete Account & Data page.

9 — Children

Children's Privacy

Receh is intended for adults managing their personal finances, not for children. The app is not designed or marketed for users under the age set by the laws that apply in your region.
